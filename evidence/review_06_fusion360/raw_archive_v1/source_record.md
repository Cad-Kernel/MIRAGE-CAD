# Fusion 360 Raw Evidence Archive v1

All existing files registered by the 400-case Step 1 audit are retained as byte-exact copies. archive_manifest_v1.json maps every original path and role to its content-addressed object, including entries for historically missing files. Empty original logs are preserved.

This includes full registered predictions, plans, source JSONLs, reference STEP and point inputs, successful prediction STEP files, metric manifests and execution logs. This is not all training data, all model weights, or all official Fusion 360 cases.
